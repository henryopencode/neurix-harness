# Agent team

Choose a Lead model and candidate models in the settings below. Describe the tasks each handles well, then save.

The Lead splits the current task into responsibilities and chooses a suitable model for each teammate. Roles are defined by the work; the same model can serve different roles. Shared tasks, member messages, and result synthesis use one Team workflow.

## Work with the Team

- Describe your task and let the Lead assign suitable teammates.
- Open **Agent team** beside the conversation title to inspect members, models, and shared tasks.
- Mention a teammate or candidate model with `@` for direct input.
- Stop one responsibility beside its name, or stop the whole Team from the input box.

Teammates share the workspace and files, with separate conversation histories. Model and strength changes apply to unused conversations; started Teams keep their selections.
