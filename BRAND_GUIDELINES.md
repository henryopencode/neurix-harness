# Neurix Harness Brand Assets

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

Use the name **Neurix Harness** and the slanted **N** symbol to identify this project. The symbol follows the Neurix visual identity and remains readable at small sizes.

- Use the full product name in page titles, installation screens, and documentation.
- Use the monochrome N in application chrome; its color follows the interface's light or dark theme.
- Use the supplied square application icons for desktop shortcuts and launchers. Their violet and pink accents follow the Neurix palette.
- Keep the symbol's proportions and leave clear space around it. Do not stretch, crop, or add effects to the wordmark.
- Describe compatibility accurately. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation with another project or company.

The [Web symbol](apps/web/public/favicon.svg), [wordmark](website/public/wordmark.svg), and [desktop icon](apps/desktop/resources/icon.svg) are the vector assets. The existing npm package names and `dsh` command remain the compatibility identifiers.
